**Faithful Companion - Your Daily Spiritual Growth Partner**

Transform your faith journey with Faithful Companion, the ultimate app for Bible study, prayer, and spiritual growth. Whether you're seeking daily inspiration, deep theological insights, or a supportive community, Faithful Companion is here to guide you every step of the way.

• Personalized daily verses tailored to your spiritual needs

• Audio Bible with natural text-to-speech
• Beautiful, distraction-free reading experience
• Offline access to your favorite verses

• Ask questions about faith, Scripture, and theology
• Get personalized prayer guidance and support
• Receive thoughtful, biblically-grounded responses

• Private and community prayer modes

**📚 BIBLE STUDY GROUPS**
• Join existing study groups or create your own
• Schedule meetings with integrated Google Meet
• Support for multiple timezones
• Recurring and one-time study sessions
• Connect with believers from around the world

**📊 TRACK YOUR SPIRITUAL JOURNEY**
• Daily streaks to build consistent habits
• XP and leveling system for motivation
• Activity tracking and insights
• Progress visualization
• Gamified spiritual growth

**🔔 SMART REMINDERS**
