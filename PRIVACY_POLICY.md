## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Personal Information You Provide

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

**Account Registration:**
- **Name:** Your full name
- **Email Address:** For account creation and communication
- **Password:** Encrypted and securely stored
- **Profile Picture:** Optional photo (if provided via Google or uploaded manually)
- **Authentication Method:** Whether you signed up via email or Google Sign-In

**Profile Preferences:**
- **Age Group:** Your age range (e.g., 18-25, 26-35, etc.)
- **Denomination:** Your Christian denomination or faith background
- **Bible Version Preference:** Your preferred Bible translation (e.g., NIV, KJV, ESV)
- **Faith Journey Stage:** Information about where you are in your spiritual journey
- **Referral Source:** How you heard about our app

**User-Generated Content:**
- **Prayer Requests:** Prayer requests you create, which may be public or private
- **Prayer Responses:** Encouragements, testimonies, and prayers you share with others
- **Prayer Notes:** Personal prayer journal entries
- **Chat Messages:** Conversations with our AI-powered faith companion
- **Reflections:** Your personal spiritual reflections and notes
- **Study Group Information:** Study groups you create or join, including group titles, descriptions, and meeting times
- **Testimonials:** Stories and experiences you choose to share

### 1.2 Information Automatically Collected

When you use our App, we automatically collect certain information:

**Device Information:**
- Device type and model
- Operating system and version (iOS/Android)
- Unique device identifiers
- Mobile network information
- Time zone settings

**Usage Information:**
- App usage statistics (session duration, frequency of use)
- Features accessed and interactions within the App
- Daily activity metrics (verses read, prayers completed, study hours)
- Streak information (current streak, longest streak)
- Content engagement (Bible passages read, reflections completed)
- App performance data and crash reports

**Notification Data:**
- Push notification tokens (for sending you notifications)
- Notification preferences and settings
- Delivery and interaction with notifications

### 1.3 Information from Third-Party Services

**Google Services:**
- When you sign in with Google, we receive:
  - Your Google account name
  - Email address
  - Profile picture
  - Google account ID
  
- When you connect Google Calendar (optional):
  - Access to create events on your calendar
  - Calendar event information for study groups you schedule
  - We do NOT read or access your personal calendar events

**AI Services:**
- We use OpenAI's GPT-4 to power our chat companion feature
- Your chat messages are processed by OpenAI's API
- Conversations are used to provide personalized spiritual guidance

## 4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information:

### 4.1 Security Measures
- **Encryption:** All data transmission uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
- **Secure Storage:** Passwords are encrypted using industry-standard hashing
- **Secure Local Storage:** Sensitive data on your device is stored using Expo Secure Store (encrypted)
- **Authentication Tokens:** Session tokens are securely stored and transmitted
- **API Security:** All API requests require authentication
- **Regular Security Updates:** We maintain up-to-date security patches

### 4.2 Your Responsibility
- Keep your password confidential and secure
- Use a strong, unique password for your account
- Sign out when using shared devices
- Report any suspicious activity immediately

### 4.3 Limitations
While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 5. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

### 5.1 Account Access and Control
- **Access Your Data:** View and download your personal information
- **Update Information:** Edit your profile, preferences, and settings at any time
- **Delete Account:** Permanently delete your account and associated data
- **Export Data:** Request a copy of your data in a portable format

### 5.2 Privacy Settings

You can control your privacy through the App:
- **Anonymous Mode:** Choose to post prayer requests anonymously
- **Public/Private Content:** Control whether your content is visible to others
- **Study Group Privacy:** Create public or private study groups
- **Profile Visibility:** Control what profile information is visible to others

### 5.3 Communication Preferences
- **Notification Settings:** Customize which notifications you receive
- **Notification Timing:** Set when you receive daily verse, prayer, and reflection notifications
- **Email Preferences:** Control promotional and informational emails
- **Disable Notifications:** Turn off push notifications entirely in your device settings

### 5.4 Google Calendar Disconnection
- You can revoke Google Calendar access at any time through Account Settings
- Disconnecting removes our access to your calendar but doesn't delete past events

### 5.5 Data Deletion

To delete your account and data:
1. Go to Profile → Account Settings
2. Select "Delete Account"
3. Confirm deletion

Upon deletion:
- Your account will be permanently removed
- Personal information will be deleted from our servers
- Public content may be anonymized rather than deleted to preserve community integrity
- Backup copies may be retained for up to 30 days for recovery purposes
